Truckin'

The first, last and best live versions of Truckin' by the Grateful Dead, with free recordings from the Internet Archive. It appears in 519 concert listings in The SetList Program, 1970 to 1995.

First time played

The Grateful Dead first played Truckin' live on August 18, 1970 at Fillmore West in San Francisco, CA.

Last time played

The last time they played it was July 6, 1995 at Riverport Amphitheatre in Maryland Heights, MO.

Best version

The top-voted version on HeadyVersion is May 19, 1974 at Portland Memorial Coliseum in Portland, OR, with 164 votes (as of October 2026). Next in the voting: 5/26/72 (115 votes) and 11/6/77 (98 votes).

About the picks

A NOTE FROM THE TAPE ROOM

What this site is

Pick a song and hear three tapes: its first known performance, its last, and the fan favorite.

Which songs are here

Over 100 songs fans love most, plus classic live sequences. It isn’t every song the Grateful Dead played. If one you love is missing, request it and it can be added. The list starts from Rolling Stone’s 100 Best Grateful Dead Songs playlist, and only performances by the original Grateful Dead are included.

First, Last, and Best

First and Last are the earliest and latest concerts where the song appears in The SetList Program. Early histories can be incomplete or disputed, so these are documented dates, not a guarantee of the true debut or final performance. A few entries rely on other references, such as JerryBase, Dead.net and a Dead Essays article, and each links to its source. A few entries have no verified date and are marked.

Best is the highest-voted version on that song’s HeadyVersion ranking. The card shows the vote count and the date we checked. It’s one community’s opinion, not the final word, and votes change over time, so this is a snapshot. If versions tie, the card says so and shows the first one listed. The live sequences use HeadyVersion’s separate sequence rankings.

Sorting

A–Z is alphabetical. Rolling Stone Top 101 follows that playlist’s order; its numbers are playlist positions, not fan votes, and songs and sequences that aren’t on that playlist come after them. Debut Date lists songs by when they were first played, earliest first.

The tapes

Audio streams from the Internet Archive. Some shows have no surviving tape, and some recordings are missing tracks, mislabeled, or temporarily unavailable. “No matched tape” means we couldn’t find a usable recording for that date. Play sequence plays a whole sequence from the same show, with a brief gap between files.

Spot a mistake?

Dates and tape matches can be wrong. Every song page has a “Report a mistake” link at the bottom, and corrections get made.
